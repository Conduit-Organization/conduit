The problem

Inference means
renting someone's cloud.

  • Your prompt leaves your device, and you trust whoever receives it
  • Millions of idle GPUs sit in laptops with no way to sell their time
  • Paying per answer needs an account, a card, and a company in the middle

How it works

The payment is
the handshake.

  • Buyer and seller find each other over a DHT — there is no server
  • A settled payment releases the seller's firewalled GPU. Nothing else does
  • The model runs on the seller's machine; weights never move
  • End to end encrypted, peer to peer — 0 bytes to any cloud

Arc · settlement

Many answers,
one settlement.

  • A payment channel opens once on-chain, then every answer is an off-chain EIP-712 voucher
  • The seller redeems them in a single claim — gas paid once, not per answer
  • On Arc, USDC is the gas token too — a seller earns and spends one asset
  • In this demo: 2 answers → 1 transaction, fee 0.0015 USDC

World · personhood

One actor can't be
a thousand customers.

  • A seller can require that a buyer is a verified unique human
  • The wallet resolves to an anonymous human ID — not a name, not a face
  • Many wallets, one person: reach is counted in people, not addresses
  • Payment alone is not enough — a funded channel from an unverified wallet is refused
